# Brag Plan: DSPM

## What is this app?
DSPM compresses multi-turn LLM conversations into typed semantic patches so constraints, decisions, and revisions can survive a limited context budget.

## The angle
Make the revision itself the hero: a webhook timeout changes from 30 seconds to 10, and DSPM keeps both the current value and its history as `10 (was 30)`. The camera travels through a warm, dimensional memory object as conversation fragments resolve into typed patches, then lands on the project's qualified benchmark results.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Two real-looking conversation lines collide: "timeout: 30 seconds" and "change it to 10 seconds." The old value rotates away; the new value locks in without erasing its lineage.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The same revision settles into a readable `[DEC] webhook timeout: 10 (was 30)` patch.
- The six wired compression stages resolve around it: dedup, slot fusion, delta encoding, causal pruning, utility scoring, and shadow selection. Do not depict adaptive per-type budgeting as shipped; the README says it is designed but not wired in this release.
- The patch becomes a compact context-memory object, connecting the visual metaphor directly to the package's real output.

## Outro / punchline
Hold the measured proof: `82.8% token reduction` and `100% critical patches selected` at a 250-token budget. Keep the benchmark scope and replication caveat legible, then resolve to `DSPM` and `pip install dspm-memory`.

## User flow worth showing
Conversation receives a revised value -> DSPM exposes the current decision with its previous value -> the compressed context is shown with benchmark evidence.

- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: premium, kinetic technical motion graphic with smooth 3D camera travel, morphing geometry, and restrained glow
- Interpretation: confident and tactile, with a warm obsidian-and-amber palette; energetic camera movement serves the revision story rather than generic spectacle.

## Duration: 15 seconds

- Display font: system sans-serif
- Body font: system sans-serif; use a system monospace stack for code

## Audio direction
- Role: understated cinematic support with sparse motion-matched accents
- Music: bundled `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`, used quietly as a rhythmic bed
- Music treatment: start at 0s, fade to a low level over 0.8s, hold beneath the visuals, fade out over the last 0.9s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset, 109.96 BPM; target strong cues at 8.74s for the compression reveal and 13.11s for the final lockup. Small graphic accents may follow nearby beats; readable copy must not be rushed.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; let existing amber bloom and object presence breathe with the music, with no waveform or visualizer graphics.
- SFX posture: sparse and warm; one reveal accent and one quiet interface landing, if they improve the motion
- Audio-coupled moments: the 8.74s pipeline resolve; the 13.11s product/CTA landing
- Restraint rule: no voiceover, no repeated ticks, and no SFX competing with the benchmark qualifier.
